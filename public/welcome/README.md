# Welcome carousel assets

Approved generated visual drafts for the ten public languages. Each square WebP is
an atlas of two columns and three rows: five landscape 3:2 scenes, unused lower-right
cell. The standalone Morocco photo overrides Arabic source cell 2. Keep atlas
coordinates and `welcome-collections.json` aligned; do not stretch or mirror people.

Arabic display order: Morocco, Mauritania, Egypt, Tunisia, Sudan (source cells
2, 1, 3, 4, 0). Shona references Zimbabwe; isiZulu references KwaZulu-Natal.
Portuguese formal and Da Banda share a collection. These are illustrative generated
scenes, not documentary photographs of identified people or verified locations.

Content-hashed filenames avoid stale image caches. WebP files are intentionally
outside the service worker's precache glob: only the selected collection is fetched.
Original Yamilook logos remain in the existing brand component, outside the images.
