# Regional onboarding photographs

Approved AI-generated concept photographs for Arabic (ar), Amharic (am), and isiZulu (zu), approved on 2026-11-05. Nine individual scenes per language: identity, birthday, welcome, avatar, bio, chat, calls, groups, stories. Extracted from the approved nine-panel sheets, without changing their content, and encoded as WebP. Fictional people; these are illustrative images, not documentary photographs.

Arabic scenes draw on Morocco, Mauritania, Egypt, Tunisia and Sudan. Amharic scenes use Ethiopian settings. isiZulu scenes use KwaZulu-Natal settings. Existing square step frames and responsive tour frames are retained. The tour uses its existing manual navigation; welcome-page carousel timing is unchanged.

All ten public languages now use nine regional scenes each. The seven added collections are Portuguese, English, French, Swahili, Lingala, Yoruba and Shona. Portuguese informal tone shares Portuguese images. Unreleased languages stay excluded.

Birthday scenes were revised for balanced representation: five men and five women, spanning young adulthood, middle age and older adulthood. Mapping: pt young man; en older woman; fr middle-aged man; ar older man; am young adult woman; sw older man; ln middle-aged woman; yo young woman; sn adult man; zu elderly woman. The images are illustrative, not assertions about real people's ages.

Registration images use responsive landscape frames. Existing validation, invitation, draft recovery and account creation behavior are retained.
